Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

Thank you for using ChemLex - Chemistry Dictionary ("App"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices regarding your information.

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

ChemLex does not require sign-up or login, and we do not collect any personal information from you. There is no account creation, no name, email, or phone number requested by the App, and no personal profile is created or stored on any server.

The App does not ask you to provide any personal information. The only data generated while using the App — such as the chemistry terms you search, your language preferences, and your bookmarked or favourite terms — is described in Section 2 below, and it stays on your device.

If you choose to contact us directly by email for support or feedback, any information you include in that email (such as your email address) is used only to respond to you and is not linked to your in-app activity.

Device Information

The App may automatically collect:

  • Device model
  • Android version
  • App version
  • Language settings
  • Crash reports
  • Anonymous diagnostics

This information is used only to improve the App, maintain stability, and diagnose technical problems.

2. Dictionary Data and Local Storage

ChemLex works fully offline. The chemistry dictionary is bundled with the App and stored locally on your device. Chemistry term lookups do not need to be sent to any server.

Your recent searches, bookmarked or favourite chemistry terms, selected languages, and other app preferences are stored locally on your device. This data is not uploaded to us or stored on our servers.

Your locally stored data remains on your device unless you clear it yourself, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.

You can remove bookmarked or favourite terms and clear recent searches from within the App where these options are available.

3. Text-to-Speech

The App may use your device's built-in text-to-speech engine to pronounce chemistry terms and definitions aloud.

Text-to-speech processing is performed using the available text-to-speech functionality on your device. ChemLex does not collect or store your audio recordings through this feature.

4. Advertising

The free version of the App may display advertisements through third-party advertising providers such as Google AdMob.

These providers may collect or process limited information, which may include:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information
  • Approximate location
  • App usage information
  • Advertising and performance information

This information may be used to provide, measure, and improve advertisements according to the advertising provider's policies and applicable consent requirements.

For more information about how Google handles information, please visit:

6. Third-Party Services
